FAQ

Common questions

How much does the session cost?
Growth Hours sessions are €5 to book. We keep it almost free intentionally, the small fee just helps make sure the people who show up are the ones who genuinely need it. In exchange for the session, we'd ask you to leave us a review on Clutch. It takes 3 minutes and helps us reach more founders like you.
Who will I speak with?
You'll speak directly with one or both of Solid Water's co-founders, Maria and Daria.
What should I prepare?
Just come with a clear description of your biggest growth challenge. The more specific, the more useful the session will be. Please answer some questions during the booking process.
What happens after the session?
We'll share any notes or resources mentioned in the call. If you'd like to keep talking, we're open to that.
Can I book another session?
Growth Hours is a one-time session. That said, founders who've joined us get preferential rates if they decide to work with Solid Water on an ongoing basis.
